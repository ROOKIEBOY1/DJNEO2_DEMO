The original bundled MP3 is unmodified. During local movie export the app decodes it, converts it to 48 kHz stereo PCM, repeats/trims it to the movie's length, applies a fade-in and fade-out of up to one second at the start and end, and encodes it as AAC-LC. Short movies use proportionally shorter fades. The source video's sound is replaced by this music. These are modifications to the music for the exported movie; no endorsement by Scott Buckley is implied.

The app displays a copyable music credit, embeds it in exported movie metadata, and displays a short credit near the end of the existing video timeline. The same source, license, attribution and modification record is bundled as `rawfile/titan-source.json`.

If an exported movie is published on YouTube, preserve the author's requested attribution in its video description, as required on the official work page. Anyone sharing the exported movie should retain the attribution, source and license link, together with the modification notice.
